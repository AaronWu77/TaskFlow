隐私政策

常见问题

  • 无法收到验证码：请检查垃圾邮件，确认邮箱地址无误，然后稍后重试。
  • 同步显示待处理：请保持网络连接并在账号页点“重新同步”。离线创建的任务会在恢复网络后继续上传。
  • 提醒没有出现：请在 iOS 系统设置中允许 TaskFlow 通知，并确认提醒时间晚于当前时间。
  • 需要删除账号：可在 App 账号页点击“删除账号”。无法登录时请通过支持邮箱联系我们。

TaskFlow Support

For help, contact support@taskflow.top. We prioritize login, sync, account deletion, and data safety requests.

FAQ

  • Verification email: check spam, confirm the email address, then retry later.
  • Pending sync: keep the app online and use Retry Sync in the account page.
  • Notifications: allow TaskFlow notifications in iOS Settings and choose a future reminder time.
  • Account deletion: use Delete Account in the app account page, or email support if you cannot sign in.
